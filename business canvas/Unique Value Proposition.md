## Unique Value Proposition

KasiPilot’s unique value proposition is:

> **KasiPilot helps informal traders manage their businesses by simply sending WhatsApp text messages or voice notes—turning everyday conversations into sales records, stock insights, and proof of business activity.**

## The value in one sentence

> **No complicated app, no manual spreadsheets, and no accounting expertise—just speak or message your business activity and KasiPilot keeps the records for you.**

## Why this is unique

Many business tools expect users to open an app, complete forms, select products, enter quantities, and understand accounting terminology. KasiPilot removes this complexity by using a conversational interface.

The trader can say:

> “I bought two boxes of bread and sold six loaves.”

KasiPilot converts this into structured information, updates the inventory, records the transactions, and provides feedback.

This approach is suited to informal traders because WhatsApp is already familiar and widely used by small businesses. Research from South Africa’s township economy indicates that WhatsApp is a preferred digital platform among surveyed businesses, partly because of cost and digital-skills constraints.[^1]

## Value for each customer

| Customer | Value received |
| :-- | :-- |
| Informal trader | Simple sales and stock management without learning complex software |
| Spaza-shop owner | Better understanding of revenue, stock, and business performance |
| Distributor | Better visibility into retailer demand and replenishment needs |
| ESD programme | More consistent information for monitoring supported businesses |
| Bank or funder | More evidence for assessing business activity and readiness |
| Trader association | A practical digital tool that can support member businesses |

## Main benefits

### 1. Simplicity

Traders use ordinary language instead of accounting forms.

### 2. Familiarity

The service operates through WhatsApp, reducing the need to download and learn a new application.

### 3. Accessibility

Voice notes allow traders to record information while serving customers or when typing is inconvenient.

### 4. Immediate usefulness

The system does not only store data. It provides useful outputs such as:

- Daily sales totals.
- Stock updates.
- Low-stock alerts.
- Best-selling products.
- Basic business summaries.


### 5. Business visibility

Consistent records help the trader understand the business and gradually build evidence of trading activity.

### 6. Pathway to opportunity

The long-term Readiness Passport can help connect reliable business records to funding, supplier support, and ESD opportunities.

## What KasiPilot is not

KasiPilot is not positioned as:

- A complicated accounting system.
- A generic chatbot.
- A replacement for a bank.
- A marketplace in the first version.
- An application that guarantees funding.

It is a **low-friction business-recording and insight tool** designed around how informal traders already communicate.

## Strong presentation version

> KasiPilot’s unique value lies in its zero-friction, WhatsApp-first approach. Informal traders can record sales and stock updates using ordinary text or voice notes instead of complicated accounting software. KasiPilot converts these conversations into structured business records, gives traders immediate insights and alerts, and gradually builds a verified history that can support access to funding, suppliers, and business-development opportunities.

## Short canvas version

> A voice- and text-enabled WhatsApp business assistant that helps informal traders record sales, manage stock, understand performance, and build credible business records without using complicated accounting software.
